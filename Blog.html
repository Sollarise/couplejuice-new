How We Started Vlogging Together (And What We'd Do Differently)

By Couplejuice · 5 min read

The first time we hit record, we argued about the opening shot for twenty minutes. The footage was shaky. The audio was terrible. But we published it anyway — and that was the best decision we made.

The Gear We Started With (And What We Actually Needed)

We thought we needed a full cinema rig. What we actually needed was a phone, a window, and the courage to be a little awkward on camera.

  • Phone: Whatever you have. Seriously.
  • Light: A window facing you beats any cheap ring light.
  • Audio: A $20 lav mic changed everything.
The best camera is the one you have with you — and the best video is the one you actually publish.

What We'd Do Differently

We'd script less. We'd laugh more. We'd stop trying to make every video a masterpiece and start treating each upload as practice.

The couples who succeed at vlogging aren't the ones with the best gear. They're the ones who keep showing up.
