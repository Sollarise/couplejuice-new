Hi, we're the couple behind Couplejuice.

We make videos together about the messy, funny, honest reality of being a couple — and share the tips and tools that help us do it.

Couplejuice started as a way to document our life together. It turned into a weekly ritual we both look forward to — setting up shots, laughing at our own bloopers, and editing late into the evening with tea in hand.

What you'll find here

On the blog, we write about vlogging, travel as a couple, and the small weekly habits that keep us connected. On our YouTube channel, you'll see the unfiltered version — no perfect lighting, no scripted lines, just us.
